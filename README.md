**WanderWise AI – Smart Budget Travel Planner**

WanderWise AI is an AI-powered travel planning platform that helps users discover destinations worldwide based on their budget, travel duration, and preferences. It simplifies trip planning by combining destination discovery, flight price comparison, accommodation recommendations, visa guidance, and personalized itinerary generation in one place.

The platform estimates the overall cost of a trip, including flights, hotels, food, local transportation, activities, and other essential expenses. It helps users explore affordable destinations, compare departure airports, identify cost-effective travel dates, and determine how long they can travel within their budget.

WanderWise AI also provides day-by-day itineraries, destination highlights, travel checklists, interactive maps, and visa and entry-requirement information to support informed travel decisions. Users can generate downloadable PDF travel guides containing their itinerary, estimated expenses, useful links, and essential travel information.

The project aims to make travel planning smarter, more accessible, and budget-friendly through AI-driven recommendations, personalized planning, and data-based cost estimation.
