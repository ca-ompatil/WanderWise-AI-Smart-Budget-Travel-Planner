# WanderWise AI – Smart Budget Travel Planner

**Website:** https://wanderwiseai.ai.studio/

## Project Description

WanderWise AI is an AI-powered travel planning platform designed to help users discover and plan affordable trips around the world. It helps travellers make smarter decisions by considering their travel budget, departure airport, trip duration, passport nationality, and personal preferences. The platform brings destination discovery, transportation cost comparison, itinerary planning, visa guidance, and travel research together in one place.

### 1. Discover Destinations Within Your Budget

This feature helps users find international and domestic destinations that fit their available budget. Users can enter their departure airport, total travel budget, trip duration, and traveller profile. The platform then displays potential destinations and estimates the overall cost of the trip, including flights, accommodation, meals, airport transfers, visa fees, and daily expenses.

Users can also filter destinations based on factors such as visa requirements, beach holidays, mountain escapes, cultural heritage, and short flight durations.

### 2. Intelligent Airport and Transport Optimizer

The Airport Optimizer helps users compare different departure airports to identify potentially cheaper travel options. Instead of considering airfare alone, it evaluates the estimated total transportation cost, including airport access, baggage charges, destination transfers, and overnight stays during connections.

For example, the platform compares travel options from Hyderabad and Chennai to Colombo and Galle, highlighting the estimated cost difference to help users understand whether travelling from an alternative airport could save money.

### 3. Personalized Itinerary and AI Travel Agent

WanderWise AI generates destination-specific travel plans with day-by-day schedules, estimated expenses, and route timings. For example, it provides a travel blueprint for Colombo and Galle, Sri Lanka, highlighting coastal rail journeys, historic Dutch architecture, beaches, and local experiences.

The itinerary page also displays the estimated total trip cost, target budget, visa status, and price-confidence information. A budget allocation breakdown helps users understand how their money may be distributed across flights, accommodation, meals, transit, and visa expenses. The AI Travel Agent provides an additional interface for travel-related assistance.

### 4. Visa Readiness and Document Checklist

This module helps travellers prepare their documents before departure. Users can select their destination and passport nationality to access relevant visa and entry-requirement guidance, official application links, and a pre-travel checklist.

A document readiness meter tracks completed tasks and remaining requirements. The platform also highlights passport validity considerations, potential scams, and the importance of using official government portals. It clarifies that final entry permission rests with the relevant authorities.

### 5. Destination Guides and Travel Blueprints

The Destination Guides section presents curated travel ideas, estimated budgets, suggested durations, and destination highlights. Examples shown include Sri Lanka, Vietnam, Nepal, Thailand, and destinations in India.

Each guide summarises key attractions, recommended travel experiences, estimated costs, and potentially suitable travel seasons. This helps users compare destinations and explore trips before committing to a detailed itinerary.

## Additional Features

The website also includes navigation options for a travel disruption monitor, group travel planning, price alerts, travel memory, currency selection, language selection, and a downloadable Travel Book. These features are designed to support a more organised and personalised travel-planning experience.

## Project Objective

The primary objective of WanderWise AI is to simplify travel planning, improve budget awareness, and help users explore more destinations with the money they have available. By combining AI-assisted planning with structured cost estimates, transportation comparisons, destination guides, and travel-readiness tools, the platform aims to make travel planning more convenient, transparent, and accessible.
